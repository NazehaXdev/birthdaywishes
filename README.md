# Romantic Birthday Surprise for Tanzu ❤️🎂

A beautiful, highly interactive, digital birthday surprise experience designed to feel like a romantic cinematic journey.

## ✨ Highlights & Features

1. **01. Opening Screen**:
   - Soft animated aurora glow, floating romantic aura, beating heart.
   - *"Tanzu ❤️"* & *"Today is your special day, Tanzu..."* with glowing *"Start the Surprise ✨"* button.
   - Ambient romantic music box melody begins automatically.

2. **02. Birthday Scene 🎂**:
   - Multi-tier animated birthday cake with strawberry/rose icing.
   - 3 interactive candles with multi-layer animated flickering flames (inspired by your custom SVG fuego).
   - Tapping each candle blows out the flame with smoke puff and sound effect.
   - Blowing out all candles triggers a confetti celebration and reveals the hidden birthday wish message: *"Make a wish... because today is all about you. ❤️"*.

3. **03. Sweet Memories ❤️**:
   - Floating constellation of glowing hearts.
   - Tapping each heart expands into a frosted glass romantic card with personal messages.
   - Tracks how many sweet notes have been discovered.

4. **04. The Love Letter 💌**:
   - Wax-sealed envelope with heart seal.
   - Tapping the seal opens the envelope flap with a 3D fold.
   - Textured cream parchment letter for Tanzu slides out smoothly.
   - Heartfelt romantic message revealed line-by-line using a typewriter animation.

5. **05. Interactive Gift 🎁**:
   - 3D gift box with yellow silk ribbons.
   - Tapping pops the lid open with a golden glow and sparkle chime.
   - Reveals *"One more little surprise... ✨"* with a sweet Romantic Lifetime Love Coupon.

6. **06. Celestial Starry Night 🌙**:
   - Deep midnight sky with a glowing crescent moon, constellations, and twinkling stardust.
   - *"Happy Birthday, Tanzu ❤️"* & *"Tanzu, thank you for being such a beautiful part of my life."*
   - *"One Last Thing..."* button reveals an intimate romantic vow under the stars.

7. **07. Grand Finale & Replay 🎆**:
   - Radiant beating 3D heart with continuous celebratory confetti fireworks.
   - *"Replay the Surprise ↻"* smoothly resets the journey so your partner can experience it anytime.

## 🛠️ How to View & Share

- **Instant Preview**: Simply double-click `index.html` to open it in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Safari, or Firefox on any PC or phone!
- **Share / Host**: You can upload these files (`index.html`, `styles.css`, `config.js`) directly to **GitHub Pages**, **Vercel**, or **Netlify** for free to send as a live link to your partner!

---

## ✏️ How to Personalize

1. **Inside the App**: Click on the name badge with the pencil icon `[Partner Name ✎]` in the top bar to change the name directly in the browser!
2. **In `config.js`**: Open [config.js](file:///c:/96/config.js) in any text editor to customize:
   - Partner's name and nickname
   - All memory card titles and text
   - Love letter paragraphs
   - Gift coupon text
   - Celestial promise message
